Italian Rule Nº 2 🍝
No parmigiano on seafood pasta.

Strong cheese hides the delicate taste of the sea — so in most of Italy, it's a firm no. Want crunch? Ask for toasted breadcrumbs, the “poor man's parmesan”.

Team cheese or team no-cheese? Choose carefully. Nonna is reading 👇
