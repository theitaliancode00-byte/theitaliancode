Italian Rule Nº 1 ☕
Never order a cappuccino after 11 a.m.

In Italy, cappuccino is a breakfast ritual. After a meal, Italians switch to a simple “caffè” — and yes, that means espresso.

Have you ever broken this rule? This is a safe space. Mostly 👇
